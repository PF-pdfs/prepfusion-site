Getting started

A short tour of Study Hub.

Not a one-time thing — this page grows every time something worth explaining ships. Right now: how Practice works, and the three small buttons under a question that are easy to miss the first time you see them.

PF-G1 · PRACTICE

Self-paced or Timed — same questions, two ways to work through them

Self-paced is the question library itself — every previous-year question, browsable by subject and chapter, at your own speed, no clock. Timed tests build a real exam-style test from the sections you pick, with GATE's own negative marking and a countdown.

Find it: the "Practice" tab in the top nav — the switch between the two modes sits right below it.

PF-G2 · INBOX

The bell icon, top right — that's your Inbox

Not a decoration — it's where things worth knowing land: a fix to a question you reported, a new video solution, updates like this page. A number on it means something's unread; open it and it's marked read.

Find it: top right of every page, the bell-shaped icon next to the dark-mode toggle.

PF-G3 · REPORT

Reporting a question — and where to actually find the button

Three small actions live on a question page, easy to walk past because they're deliberately quiet. Two live in different places, not one:

Get to a question: "Practice" (top nav) → "Self-paced" → pick a subject → pick a chapter → open any question. (Reviewing a finished Timed test drops you into questions the same way.)
⚑ Report problem with question — right below the question and its options, on any previous-year question (Self-paced or reviewing a finished Timed test). Not shown during a running timed test — reporting against a live clock helps nobody.
⚑ Report problem with solution and 🎬 Request a video solution — open "Show solution" first. Both sit just below the solution itself, since you can only judge a solution (or ask for a video walkthrough of one) once you've actually read it.
Live now On a question
PF-G4 · TRACKING

Bookmark, Visited and Rate — the three small icons above a question

Same top-right row as the calculator button, on every question. All three are for you, not us — nothing here gets reported anywhere.

🔖 Bookmark — save this exact question to come back to later, from your Profile.
👁 Visited — marks a question as opened, so a chapter's progress tracker knows what you've already been through. Hidden during a running timed test.
Rate (Easy / Medium / Hard) — your own difficulty judgement, sits just above the question body. Also hidden during a running timed test — it's meant to be answered after you've actually worked the question, not against the clock.
