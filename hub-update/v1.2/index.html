What's new

Study Hub is no longer just a question bank.

The headline this time: full courses, watchable and tracked, right alongside Practice. Five updates, all live right now — here's exactly what's new and how to get the most out of each one.

PF-08 · STUDY

Full courses, watchable here, with a real tracker

Every GATE and VLSI course, chapter by chapter — Free courses play in full right here; Paid ones show the real syllabus as a preview. The tracker weighs progress by actual lecture length, not just boxes ticked, and PYQ chapters get their own separate tracker underneath.

Find it: the "Study" tab, next to Practice in the top nav.
Live now Study tab
PF-09 · TAILORED

Four quick questions, and Study tunes itself to you

First time you open Study, it asks your branch, target GATE year, preparation stage, and VLSI placement track — so the catalog is ordered and weighted for the exam you're actually sitting. All four stay editable any time afterward.

Find it: it appears automatically the first time you open Study; change answers later in the account menu → "My details."
New Onboarding
PF-10 · DASHBOARD

One place for your whole preparation, at a glance

A new tab pulls your progress together across GATE and VLSI, free and paid, lectures and PYQ chapters — a single view instead of hunting for it inside course pages. Split out of "My details" on purpose, since a settings form and a progress report are two different reasons to come back.

Find it: the "Dashboard" tab, next to Study; also in the account menu.
New Dashboard
PF-11 · CATALOG

Free or Paid — simpler labels, and no dead ends on notes

"Premium" is now plainly Paid; the old "Capsule course" pill is gone too. General Aptitude now lives in one place, not split across a free and paid twin. And any course without notes of its own — free or paid — now points straight to our Telegram group instead of a blank space.

Find it: any course page in Study — look for the badge and the notes links.
Live now Study catalog
PF-12 · GUIDE

The Guide page now actually shows you where things are

Updated with exact steps to reach the report buttons (not just what they do), a live-rendered example question so you can see the row before you go looking for it, and a clear callout for the Inbox bell — plus Bookmark, Visited and Rate, explained in one place.

Find it: the account menu → "Guide", or the Inbox's welcome message.
