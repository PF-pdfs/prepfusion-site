What's new

Your Study Hub just leveled up.

Seven updates, all live right now. Nothing to install, nothing to configure — just open the app and they're there. Here's exactly what's new and how to get the most out of each one.

PF-01 · PRACTICE

Timed mock tests, built your way

Pick your subjects, chapters, difficulty, and question count — get a real timed test with the exact same negative-marking rules as the actual GATE exam. Finish it and get a full report: accuracy by subject, accuracy by difficulty, and a clean PDF you can keep.

Find it: the "Practice" tab, right next to Study Hub in the top nav.
Beta Timed test
PF-02 · CHECK

Know instantly if you got it right

Select an answer on any question while you're browsing — not just during a test — and hit "Check answer" for an immediate green or red. Miss it, and the right option lights up too, so you always see what you should have picked.

Find it: it just works — open any question with a clean answer key.
Live now Answer check
PF-03 · CALC

The GATE calculator, built in

The same virtual scientific calculator used in the real exam interface — full trig, log, memory, the works — now sits one tap away on every single question. Drag it wherever's convenient and keep solving.

Find it: the calculator icon next to any question's marks/type.
Live now On every Q
PF-04 · HISTORY

Every test you've taken, in one place

"My reports" keeps a running history of every Practice test — pick up an unfinished one right where you left off, or reopen a finished one to review the full breakdown any time.

Find it: your account menu → "My reports."
Live now Test history
PF-05 · INBOX

A proper home for updates

Notifications is now Inbox — the place where things worth knowing land: fixes to a question you reported, a new video solution, and updates like this one. One glance at the badge tells you if there's something new.

Find it: you're reading this from it right now.
Live now Renamed
PF-06 · NIGHT

Dark mode, fixed for real diagrams

Circuit diagrams and figures used to sit on the page as a glaring white rectangle at night. They now invert cleanly with the theme, so late-night sessions actually look right.

Find it: the moon icon, top right — same as always, just better now.
Fixed Diagrams
PF-07 · PROGRESS

See what you've already worked through

Every question you open now gets quietly marked as visited — separate from bookmarks, just a simple "have I done this one" trail. Flip it either way yourself, and use "Unvisited only" to jump straight to what's left in a chapter.

Find it: next to the bookmark icon on any question, and the "Unvisited only" filter in a chapter's question list.
New Track progress

That's v1.1. One more thing worth knowing: My Profile is there whenever you want it — a couple of minutes filling it in and the site gets noticeably better at working the way you actually prep.

We build this in the open, mostly from what you tell us is broken or missing — if something here doesn't work the way you expected, report it the same way you always have, right from the question.
