What's new

The board pays real courses — and the site remembers every question you miss.

The biggest release yet. Finish high on the monthly board and win a paid PrepFusion course, free. Underneath that: four daily arenas, questions you get wrong served back until you get them right, a streak that survives a bad day, a marks table for the whole 1987–2026 library, and a log of every question you have ever answered. Nine updates, all live right now.

PF-13 · REWARDS

The monthly board pays real courses now

Finish the month near the top and you win a paid PrepFusion course, free — not points, not a badge. Five products are on the table, and the higher you finish the bigger the one you are in line for.

  • Test Series
  • Subject Course
  • Combo Course
  • Placement Course
  • Full Course
Find it: Leaderboard → “What you can win.” Which rank gets which is decided each month and announced with the results. One thing to sort out in advance — sign in here with the same email you registered on prepfusion.in, or the coupon can't reach you.
New Leaderboard
PF-14 · ARENAS

Four timed arenas a day, and they stay open

Foundation, Base Camp, Electronics and Electrical each get their own daily arena — a fresh timed set rolling automatically every evening. Base Camp is the newest: aptitude and engineering maths, the part of the paper every branch sits. Everyone on a track gets the same arena, so the standings are a real head-to-head.

Find it: the Contests tab. A new set rolls at 6 PM IST and runs until the next one opens — so the arena you find at 2 AM is still last evening's, standings intact. Weekdays 10 questions in 30 minutes, weekends 20 in an hour. Standings are readable without entering.
Live now Contests
PF-15 · STREAK

One question a day, and a streak that survives a bad day

Daily Focus gives you one question a day per track — do it and your streak grows, and a points booster kicks in (×1.1 at 3 days, ×1.25 at 7, up to ×1.5 at 30). Reach 3, 7, 14 or 30 days and you also earn a streak shield: miss a day and one is spent automatically, silently, and the run carries on.

Find it: the Daily Focus card on the Contests page, above the arenas; your streak, booster and shields sit on the Dashboard. A shield covers exactly one day, so a week away still costs you the streak — but one bad Tuesday doesn't. A shielded day shows on the heat map as an outline rather than a fill, so the record stays honest.
New Daily Focus
PF-16 · RECOVERY

The questions you get wrong come back

Every question you miss — in a timed test, an arena, Daily Focus or Self-paced — is filed and served back to you until you get it right. Getting one wrong is no longer something that happens and then vanishes.

Find it: Practice → Timed tests → drill only what you got wrong. One fairness note: a question you miss can still be recovered, but it no longer pays first-time points when you later get it right. Clicking through the options until one lands was paying the same as knowing the answer.
New Practice
PF-17 · WEIGHTAGE

See exactly what each subject has been worth

Marks Distribution is built from the whole question library, 1987–2026: how many marks a subject has actually carried in one real sitting of the exam, year by year. Drill it Subject → Branch → Chapter to find the heaviest chapter in whatever you are about to revise.

Find it: the Marks distribution card on the home page. Years that ran several sessions are divided down to a single paper, so a three-session year doesn't silently outweigh a one-session year.
New Library
PF-18 · LOG

Every question you've ever answered, in one place

The question log holds the lot — timed test, arena, Daily Focus, Self-paced, plus everything you saved — filed where it lives in the library: subject, then branch, then chapter. Every rung says how many questions sit under it and how many you got wrong, so it reads as a map of your gaps rather than a diary.

Find it: the tick-list icon in the top bar, or the account menu. Everything starts folded; open a chapter to see its questions, and a question to read it right there without leaving the page. Bookmarks work the same way now.
New Your work
PF-19 · TELEGRAM

Get nudged where you'll actually see it

Link your Telegram and the site can reach you off-site: a reminder when the day's arena opens, your placing once the standings settle, and a nudge when a streak is about to lapse. Nothing is posted anywhere public — it is a private message to you, and you can unlink in one click.

Find it: account menu → Telegram. One button links the chat.
New Notifications
PF-20 · BOOKMARKS

Attempt your bookmarks as a test, at your own pace

Bookmarked a question to come back to? Start a timed test built entirely from your own bookmarks — and now choose the pace before you begin, exam speed or something gentler while you are still learning the material. The countdown is built from what you pick.

Find it: account menu → Bookmarks → “Attempt as timed test.” Bookmarks are grouped by subject, branch and chapter, so a long list stays a short one until you open it.
New Bookmarks
PF-21 · REFRESH

A cleaner site, a leaderboard, and a profile worth sharing

A front-end pass across the whole site — sharper layout, tidier icons, smoother mobile navigation. The Leaderboard is live, ranked GATE-style so an equal score is an equal rank. Your public profile is a real page now with a shareable stats card, on a username and never an email. And everything about your own account sits on one Dashboard, so a figure appears once instead of twice with two framings.

Find it: everywhere — the Leaderboard tab, and account menu → My profile, which carries your public profile too.
