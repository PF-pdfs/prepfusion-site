What's new

Practice got competitive — and the front end got a facelift.

Daily contest arenas, a question every day that keeps a streak, and a UI pass across the whole site. Four updates, all live right now — here's exactly what's new and how to get the most out of each one.

PF-13 · ARENAS

A new timed arena every day, three tracks

Common Core, Electronics and Electrical each get their own daily contest arena — a fresh, timed set of questions rolling automatically every evening. Everyone on a track sees the same arena, so the standings are a real head-to-head, not just a personal best.

Find it: the "Contests" tab in the top nav. A new arena opens at 6 PM every day.
Live now Contests
PF-14 · STREAK

One question a day, a streak that pays off

Daily Focus gives you one question a day per track — do it and your streak grows, keep it alive and a points booster kicks in (1.1×, then 1.2× on a longer streak), shown right where you earn points. And you'll actually see the points land: a short animation now plays every time you score.

Find it: the Daily Focus card on the home page; your streak and current booster sit right next to it.
New Daily Focus
PF-15 · BOOKMARKS

Attempt your bookmarks as a real timed test

Bookmarked a question to come back to later? You can now start a timed test built entirely from your own bookmarks — same test interface, same timer and review screen you already know from Practice.

Find it: the account menu → "Bookmarks" → "Attempt as timed test."
New Bookmarks
PF-16 · REFRESH

A cleaner look, and a leaderboard to check

A front-end pass across the site — sharper layout, tidier icons, smoother mobile navigation between Practice and Timed tests. And the Leaderboard is live, so you can see where you stand against everyone else on your track.

Find it: everywhere — and the "Leaderboard" tab specifically.
Live now Site-wide
